     rapid double-clicks or `Enter` key presses before UI re-render.
   - Wrapped form inputs in `<fieldset disabled={...}>` with `pointer-events-none`
     on the submit button while posting, and added a 25-second payload
     fingerprint deduplication cache on both client and server (`30s` window).
   - Fixed a `ReferenceError` in standalone `handleBillPaymentSubmit`'s
     `upsertBillerFromActivity` call so the form always resets cleanly after
     posting.

[v2.8.9] - 2026-11-11 — Recomputed Days Delayed & Remaining Lead Time (7-Working-Day Payment Processing Standard)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Recomputed Days Delayed & Lead Time Left (`7 Working Days` Processing Requirement):
   - Fixed the `Late` and `Overdue` calculation in both **Payment Status** (under
     `Request ID`) and **Account History Report** (`Movement / Delay` column).
   - Previously, `Late (Xd)` displayed the raw working-day difference between
     Filing Date and Due Date (`weekdaysBeforeDue`), which caused a submission
     with 5 working days left before Due Date to display `Late (5d)` and a
     submission with only 1 working day left to display `Late (1d)`.
   - Since **7 working days** are required to process payment:
     * **Lead Time Left Till Due Date** = `leadDaysLeft` (working days between
       Filing Date and Due Date).
     * **Days Delayed** = `7 - leadDaysLeft` (for late submissions with `< 7`
       working days lead time) or `7 + weekdaysPastDue` (when submitted on or
       after Due Date).
   - Badges now clearly display both values: e.g., `Late 2d (5d left)` when filed
     5 working days before Due Date, `Late 6d (1d left)` when filed 1 working
     day before Due Date, and `Overdue · 7d late (0d left)` when filed on Due Date.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Fixed Duplicate Row Appended When Editing Bill or Enrollment Details:
   - Root Cause: Saving billing edits (`updateBillingDetails`) or enrollment
     edits (`updateEnrollmentDetails`) previously dispatched the payload to BOTH
     the Bill Sub WebApp script (`USER_SCRIPT_URL`, which edits the row in place)
     AND the Approver's legacy Drive upload script (`PAYMENT_SCRIPT_URL` /
     `DEFAULT_APPS_SCRIPT_URL`), which lacked the `updateBillingDetails` handler
     and fell through to its `REQ-` / `ENR-` new-submission `appendRow` block.
   - Fix: Routed `updateBillingDetails` and `updateEnrollmentDetails` exclusively
     to `USER_SCRIPT_URL` (`DEFAULT_USER_SCRIPT_URL`) across React, Express
     server routes, and Standalone HTML so edits strictly update the existing
     row in place and never append a duplicate row copy.
   - Added automatic `requestId` deduplication during live Google Sheet GViz
     sync so any previously created `"No File Attached"` duplicate row is
     automatically collapsed into the original entry.
