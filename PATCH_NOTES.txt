[v3.2.0] - 2026-11-16 — New Report: Upcoming Due Date Projections & Lapsed Payments Tracker (`REP_DUE_LAPSED` from `IR_RFPReq`, `Billers`, & `Request Bill Payments`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Live `IR_RFPReq` ImportRange Integration (`fetchIrRfpReqRecords` in `billPayment.ts`):
   - Fetches all 5,488+ historical and current payment records from the `IR_RFPReq`
     sheet tab in `Bill Sub WebApp Requests` (`1WI4CA4hy1s3oyCZLvgDVA1SeJSj9gN2BfLLeRpUmZDA`).
   - Automatically recovers alphanumeric/hyphenated Water & Utility account numbers
     from `Col D (Helper: Month_Year_AccountNumber)` whenever Google Visualization
     API types `Col A` as numeric, achieving 99%+ account matching across all 361
     registered accounts in the `Billers` catalog.
2. Upcoming Due Date Projections from Last Known Payments:
   - Determines each registered `Billers` account's typical billing due day-of-month
     from its latest valid `Due Date` in `IR_RFPReq` / `Request Bill Payments` and
     projects its upcoming due date for the selected Target Month & Year.
   - Flags accounts with countdown indicators (`Due in Xd`, `Projected Due Today`,
     `Past Projected by Xd`, or `Cycle Fulfilled`).
3. Lapsed / Skipped Monthly Payments Detection:
   - Detects and flags accounts that have skipped one or more monthly billing cycles
     between their last known payment record and the target billing cycle (e.g., if
     due for November 2026 but the last known record was July 2026, flags as
     `LAPSED (3 MONTHS)` and explicitly lists `Missing: Aug 2026, Sep 2026, Oct 2026`).
4. Automatic Fulfillment Resolution:
   - Upcoming Projected Due, Past Projected Due, and Lapsed Payments strictly
     display and count ONLY unfulfilled accounts (and default to the
     "All Unfulfilled" view on load).
   - If a payment request for the target billing/due month has been submitted in
     `Request Bill Payments` (active/non-cancelled/non-rejected) or recorded in
     `IR_RFPReq`, the account is automatically marked as **`FULFILLED`**
     (`FULFILLED • PAID`, `FULFILLED • WITH RFP`, or `FULFILLED • SUBMITTED`).
   - Includes a per-account **Timeline** drill-down modal, direct **`+ File RBP`**
     prefill shortcut, interactive KPI filter cards, and full CSV export.

1. Fixed Google Drive "Files Shared With Me" Emails on Every Upload:
   - **Root Cause**: `OneTimePayment_Isolated_AppsScript.js` previously called
     `file.addEditor(ownerEmail)` (`lasarmiento.apec@gmail.com`) on each of the 3
     uploaded attachments (`RequestLetter`, `SOA`, `MgmtApprovedRLF`). In Google Apps
     Script, `DriveApp.File.addEditor(email)` unconditionally triggers a Google Drive
     "File shared with you" notification email on every call.
   - **Fix (`OneTimePayment_Isolated_AppsScript.js` v2.0 Production + REST API v3)**:
     * Removed `file.addEditor(...)` completely from `OneTimePayment_Isolated_AppsScript.js`.
       Because the script is deployed under `lasarmiento.apec@gmail.com` (`Execute as: Me`)
       and writes directly into personal folder `1u7lR6OCbzoYfmLXe4TGeQ2LyGJiLHDa5`,
       the uploaded files are already owned by your account inside that folder, and
       `file.setSharing(DriveApp.Access.ANYONE_WITH_LINK, DriveApp.Permission.VIEW)`
       enables link viewing silently with **zero email notifications**.
     * Added `?sendNotificationEmail=false` to the client-side Google Drive REST API v3
       permission fallback in `oneTimePaymentService.ts`.
2. Full Production Promotion of One-Time Bill Payment (`Module 5`):
   - Promoted Group 8 in Mod Access from `8. One-Time Bill Payment (Experimental)` to
     **`8. One-Time Bill Payment & Status` (`Module 5`)**.
   - Enabled `EXP_ONETIME_PAYMENT` by default across **all active user roles**
     (`Administrator`, `Sub-Administrator`, and `Level 1`–`Level 10`) so all portal
     users can file and track One-Time Bill Payments.
   - Added **One-Time Utility Bill Payment** and **View One-Time Payment Status**
     quick-action cards directly to the **Overview Dashboard (`UTILITY SERVICES & WORKFLOWS`)**.
   - Scoped standard users to their own submitted requests in `One-Time Status` while
     giving Admins/Sub-Admins an **`All Requests` / `My Requests`** scope toggle.
3. Production Upload Enforcement & Clean Authoritative Google Sheet Sync:
   - Set default upload enforcement mode (`getOneTimeUploadMode()`) to **`'required'`**
     so all 3 attachments (`Approved Request Letter`, `Current Billing / SOA`, and
     `Management Approved RLF`) are required on submission by default.

[v2.8.9] - 2026-11-11 — Recomputed Days Delayed & Remaining Lead Time (7-Working-Day Payment Processing Standard)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Recomputed Days Delayed & Lead Time Left (`7 Working Days` Processing Requirement):
   - Fixed the `Late` and `Overdue` calculation in both **Payment Status** (under
     `Request ID`) and **Account History Report** (`Movement / Delay` column).
   - Previously, `Late (Xd)` displayed the raw working-day difference between
     Filing Date and Due Date (`weekdaysBeforeDue`), which caused a submission
     with 5 working days left before Due Date to display `Late (5d)` and a
     submission with only 1 working day left to display `Late (1d)`.
   - Since **7 working days** are required to process payment:
     * **Lead Time Left Till Due Date** = `leadDaysLeft` (working days between
       Filing Date and Due Date).
     * **Days Delayed** = `7 - leadDaysLeft` (for late submissions with `< 7`
       working days lead time) or `7 + weekdaysPastDue` (when submitted on or
       after Due Date).
   - Badges now clearly display both values: e.g., `Late 2d (5d left)` when filed
     5 working days before Due Date, `Late 6d (1d left)` when filed 1 working
     day before Due Date, and `Overdue · 7d late (0d left)` when filed on Due Date.
